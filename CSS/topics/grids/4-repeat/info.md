## repeat()
    reduces manual writing
    saves time but makes sense only when all widths/height are equal 

## how to use:
    grid-template-columns:  1fr 1fr 1fr 1fr 1fr 1fr , can also be written as:
    grid-template-columns: repeat(6,1fr)
